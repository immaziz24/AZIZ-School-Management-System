Roles & permissions

Seven roles. One platform.

Choose a role to see exactly what that person can do.

Role

Platform Admin

The SaaS operator. Runs the whole platform: every school, plan and invoice from one control room.

  • See every school with plan, status, usage and last activity
  • Create plans: trial, basic, standard, premium, with limits and prices
  • Raise invoices, mark them paid, track revenue and unpaid dues
  • Suspend or reactivate a school (it turns read-only, nothing is deleted)
  • Extend trials and set grace periods and renewal dates
  • Answer school support tickets
  • Approve custom-domain and data export or deletion requests
  • Switch platform features on or off
  • Log disaster-recovery drills and keep legal holds
Role

School Owner

Full control of the school. The school’s leader: configures everything and sees every record.

  • Create the school, academic years, classes and subjects
  • Add students, staff and family logins; import from CSV
  • Set fees, concessions, record payments, see defaulters
  • Run payroll, staff directory, trainings and leave approvals
  • Set up exams, grading rules, approve and publish results
  • Build the timetable, calendar, notices and school website content
  • Manage library, transport routes, inventory, assets and vendors
  • Track expenses, sales, finance summary and certificates
  • Invite teachers, principals and co-owners; switch members on or off
  • See the activity log, take backups and export data
  • Manage the plan, invoices, support tickets, custom domain and data requests
Role

Principal

View-only oversight. Sees the whole school at a glance without the risk of changing records.

  • View attendance, results, fees and finance summaries
  • See the “needs attention” list: low attendance, dues, open complaints
  • Review marks and approve results for publishing
  • Monitor teacher workload and substitution cover
  • Read complaints, behaviour records and meeting minutes
  • Print or download report cards, certificates and reports
  • Everything is read-only, so nothing can be edited by mistake
Role

Teacher

Focused classroom tools. Only their own classes and subjects, nothing extra to get lost in.

  • Mark daily student attendance in seconds
  • Enter and correct marks for their class and subject
  • Set homework, assessments and review student submissions
  • See their own timetable and school calendar
  • Write lesson plans and a daily diary
  • Take substitution cover when a colleague is absent
  • Request leave and mark their own attendance
  • Send class notices and read school notices
Role

Parent

One login for every child. Clear, current information about each child, on any phone.

  • Switch between children with one login
  • See attendance and low-attendance alerts
  • View fee dues, payments and receipts
  • Read homework, notices, calendar and events
  • See results and report cards
  • Book parent-teacher meeting slots
  • Send messages to the school and register for events
  • Submit assessment responses
Role

Student

Their own learning space. Everything a student needs for the day, nothing they should not see.

  • View today’s timetable and school calendar
  • See homework and submit work online
  • Check attendance and results
  • Take online assessments set by teachers
  • Read notices and register for school events
Role

Visitors & Prospective Parents

The public face of the school. No login needed: the school website works as the front door for admissions.

  • Read about the school: about, facilities, achievements, gallery
  • See notices and upcoming events
  • Send an admission enquiry in one minute
  • Register for open days and events
  • Find contact details and location
