Questions

Answers, before you ask

Do I need to install anything?

No. AZIZ runs in any modern browser on phone, tablet or computer. Nothing to install or maintain.

Can parents and teachers use it too?

Yes. You invite them by email and each person sees only what their role allows.

Is my school's data private?

Every school has its own private space. Other schools can never see your records.

Can I move my existing data?

Yes. Upload students and staff from a spreadsheet, and our team can help with larger migrations.
