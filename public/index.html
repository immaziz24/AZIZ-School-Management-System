                                                                        Data Backup

                                                                        Downloads a copy of the school's records as one file. It contains private student, family and staff details: keep it somewhere safe and do not share it.

                                                                        School data pack

                                                                        Downloads every list as its own spreadsheet (CSV) file, packed in one ZIP file you can open in Excel. Use it when you leave AZIZ or want your own copy. It also works after a school is cancelled.

                                                                        Restore from a nightly backup

                                                                        AZIZ saves a copy of your records every night and keeps the last 14. If records were deleted or changed by mistake, pick a copy, check what a restore would change, then restore only the lists you choose. A restore never deletes anything, and a safety copy of the chosen lists is saved first. Sign-in accounts, the activity log and settings are never restored.
